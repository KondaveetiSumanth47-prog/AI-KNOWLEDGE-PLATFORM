Education

Education is the process of acquiring knowledge, skills, values, and understanding through learning and teaching.

Active learning is an approach where students participate directly in the learning process instead of only listening to lectures.

Project-based learning allows students to learn by working on practical projects. It can improve problem-solving, teamwork, communication, and application of knowledge.

Assessment is used to measure a student's learning and progress. Common forms include formative assessment and summative assessment.

Formative assessment is performed during the learning process to identify areas for improvement. Summative assessment is generally performed at the end of a course or learning period.

Online learning uses digital platforms to provide educational content through computers and other connected devices.

Blended learning combines traditional classroom teaching with online learning resources.
